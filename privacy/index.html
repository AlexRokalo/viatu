Privacy Policy

Viatu: Travel Map & Tracker

SupportPrivacy PolicyTerms of Use

Effective date: October 4, 2026

Viatu ("the app") is made by Alexey Rokalo ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles and why. In short: your travel data stays on your iPhone, we have no accounts and no servers of our own, and we never sell your data.

Your travel data stays on your device

The trips, countries, cities, dates and notes you add are stored only on your iPhone. We do not have a server and cannot see this data. It is included in your device backups according to your own iCloud or computer backup settings.

Photos

If you choose to find trips in your photos, the app asks for access to your photo library and reads the date and location of your photos on your device to suggest trips. Photos and their locations are never uploaded or shared. You can change or revoke this access at any time in iOS Settings.

Services we use

Mapbox (maps)

The world map is provided by Mapbox. To display it, your device downloads map data from Mapbox, which receives technical information such as your IP address and device type. Mapbox may also collect anonymous usage data to improve its maps; you can turn this off from the ⓘ button on the map. Mapbox does not receive your trips. See the Mapbox Privacy Policy.

RevenueCat and Apple (purchases)

Purchases are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to check whether you have Premium. RevenueCat receives a random, anonymous app user ID, your purchase history and basic device and app information. It does not receive your name, email or payment details, and it does not receive your trips. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy and Apple's Privacy Policy.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, no sign-up.
  • No advertising and no ad tracking.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No selling or renting of your data.

Your choices

You can export your trips as a CSV file or delete all data in Profile → Your data. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device. To ask for your purchase records held by RevenueCat to be deleted, write to us at the address below.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
