Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. Viatu has no sign-up. Your trips are stored on your phone.

How does finding trips in my photos work?

With your permission, Viatu reads the dates and locations of the photos on your phone and suggests the trips behind them. This happens entirely on your device: no photo or location is uploaded, and you approve every trip before it is added.

Why does the map need an internet connection?

The world map is streamed from Mapbox. Your trips, stats and achievements are on your device and work without a connection.

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account (iPhone) or Google account (Android) you bought with, open Profile and tap Restore purchases. Purchases do not move between iPhone and Android.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by the store you bought from. You keep Premium until the end of the period you paid for.

  • iPhone: open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, choose Viatu and tap Cancel Subscription. See Apple's guide: Cancel a subscription from Apple.
  • Android: open the Google Play Store app, tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, choose Viatu and tap Cancel subscription. See Google's guide: Cancel a subscription on Google Play.

How do I get a refund?

Payments go through the store, so refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com for iPhone, or by Google Play for Android: see Google Play refunds.

Can I export or delete my data?

Yes. In Profile → Your data you can export your trips as a CSV file or delete all data. Deleting the app also removes everything it stored on your phone.
